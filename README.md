# Sanjeevani

## Purpose and status

`TB_Flipchart_Video_Script.pptx` is a draft script, storyboard and visual reference presentation for the Sanjeevani project. It supports review and development of a TB flipchart and two language versions of an animated awareness video commissioned by CARE Nepal in coordination with the National Health Education, Information and Communication Center (NHEICC), Department of Health Services (DoHS).

This README relates the current presentation to `Final_ToR_TB_Flipchart_Animated_Video_Sanjeevani.pdf`. The Terms of Reference (ToR) define the assignment requirements. The presentation proposes content and creative treatments for review. Neither this README nor the presence of material in the deck establishes technical or production approval.

**Current status:** draft for community and technical review. The presentation is a development document, not the final print-ready flipchart or finished video.

## Project context and audience

CARE Nepal implements Sanjeevani with SPARSHA Nepal in Rautahat District, Madhesh Province. The brief seeks communication materials that support early case detection and diagnosis, treatment adherence and continuity of care, and reduced stigma and harmful social norms.

The flipchart is intended for frontline health workers and community facilitators. The video is intended for community screening, training and digital/social media use. Both should communicate clearly to people with varying literacy levels through accessible illustrations and simple spoken language.

## Key decisions before production

1. **Sanjeevani framing.** The brief identifies Sanjeevani as the project name. The deck also presents it as a medicine, a healing herb and a character. Slide 54 already flags the medicine-name issue. CARE Nepal and NHEICC/DoHS should agree the wording and visual metaphor before translation or animation, including whether the herb-treatment imagery could mislead viewers.
2. **Technical messaging and service claims.** Request technical validation of the simplified explanations, symptom and testing dialogue, treatment and preventive-treatment distinctions, household eligibility, and claims about free services at the nearest health post. This README records review needs, not clinical validation.
3. **Local relevance.** Review the mountain and herb-garden imagery, clothing, characters and health-post settings against the intended Rautahat/Madhesh audience. Confirm which images are approved references and which need replacement.
4. **Script completeness.** Scene 5 contains a descriptive paragraph but no clearly separated final narration. Complete spoken lines, visual actions and audio cues for every scene before translation and timing.
5. **Artwork and branding.** Check embedded image text, medicine packaging, signage and logos for accuracy and legibility. Establish a consistent character treatment across the flipchart and video.
6. **Scope and accessibility.** Agree the flipchart sheet count, illustration count, language versions and sign language provider. Keep space for interpretation in all scene compositions.

## Review and production workflow

The following workflow translates the ToR's review requirements into practical steps:

1. Review the script and creative concept with CARE Nepal and NHEICC/DoHS. Resolve the decisions above and agree the sheet plan.
2. Revise the content using consolidated feedback. Obtain technical validation and approval of the agreed scripts and visual direction.
3. Develop the A4 flipchart layout and the full storyboard/animatic. Prepare and validate Nepali and the agreed Maithili/Bajhika scripts.
4. Share the draft flipchart and storyboard/animatic for review. Incorporate consolidated comments before full production.
5. Produce the animation, narration and sign language interpretation. Submit rough cuts for review and revision.
6. Obtain final approvals and deliver the print-ready flipchart, both videos and complete source files by the ToR deadline.

The ToR identifies **Neera Thakur, Lead — Health System and Rights, CARE Nepal**, for programmatic and technical coordination, and **Amit Pradhan, Lead, SIP**, for design and branding. NHEICC/DoHS provides TB technical input and approval. The consultant coordinates review meetings, including NHEICC Technical Working Group meetings, and incorporates consolidated feedback. See ToR page 3.
